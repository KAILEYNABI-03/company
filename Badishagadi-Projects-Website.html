Who we are

A South African women-owned supply business with a service-first approach.

Badishagadi Projects (Pty) Ltd is a procurement and supply business serving public and private sector clients. We source a wide range of products and consumables, helping clients procure across multiple categories through a single supplier.

Through supplier relationships and efficient procurement processes, we aim to provide cost-effective solutions tailored to each client’s operational requirements.

Our vision

To become one of South Africa’s most trusted and preferred suppliers of consumables, equipment and procurement solutions across the public and private sectors.

Our mission

To provide high-quality products, excellent customer service and efficient supply chain solutions, while building long-term relationships through reliability, integrity and value for money.
